Amp & Effects

Plug in, press Amp off to power up, and play. Drag a knob up or down (Shift for fine control), use the arrow keys, or double-click to reset it.

Signal flow: Gate → Compressor → Overdrive → AMP → Cabinet → Chorus → Delay → Reverb → Out

How to connect your guitar
  • Best: a USB guitar interface. Pick it in the input menu at the top.
  • Quick: your computer's mic placed near an acoustic or a small practice amp.
  • No guitar handy? Switch the input to Audio file and load a dry (DI) guitar recording to dial in tones.
  • Use headphones. Speakers plus a live mic will feed back.
  • Chrome or Edge usually gives the lowest latency. Close other audio apps if you hear crackles.

Tuner
