# Riffbox

A guitar amp and practice studio that runs entirely in your browser. No install, no account, no server.

**Live app:** https://skoodles.github.io/riffbox/

## Features

- **Amp & effects**: noise gate, compressor, overdrive, 5 amp models, 5 speaker cabinets, chorus, delay and reverb. Includes built-in presets, and you can save your own.
- **Tuner**: chromatic tuner with standard, drop D, DADGAD, open G/D, half-step-down and bass tunings, plus reference tones.
- **Metronome**: accents, subdivisions, tap tempo and a speed trainer.
- **Chord library**: 42 playable chord diagrams and a progression player.
- **Recorder**: records your processed tone. Takes can be downloaded.

## Getting started

1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge (lowest latency).
2. Plug in a USB guitar interface, or use your computer's mic.
3. **Wear headphones.** Speakers plus a live mic will feed back.
4. Click **Amp off** to power on and allow microphone access.

## Privacy

Audio never leaves your device. Presets are saved in your browser's local storage only.
